Frequently asked questions

Does the stopwatch beep when I press Start and Stop?

Yes. Start and Stop use different audible cues so you can tell which action occurred without looking at the display.

Can I change the beep and its volume?

Yes. Open the sound settings cog to choose a theme, select a volume from 1 to 5, and preview both cues.

Can I use keyboard shortcuts?

Yes. Press Space to start or stop the timer, and press R to reset it to 00:00.00.
